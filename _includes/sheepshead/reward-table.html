Rewards in the bootstrap phase

Every reward is in units of game score ÷ 12, so one game point is 0.083. A decision's reward is the sum of everything below that applies to it.

1. Final score every phase

The player's score for the hand ÷ 12, given on their last action. This is the only reward after the bootstrap phase.
